About Kist Caterers

Rooted in the heart of Maskasath, Itwari — redefining grand hospitality and authentic flavors.

Whether you are planning a vibrant Haldi-Mehndi ritual at home, an extravagant royal wedding banquet, or a high-volume religious Mahaprasad for 50,000 devotees, Kist Caterers bridges authentic taste with industrial-scale execution. Supported by specialized master chefs (karagirs), modern steam-cooking infrastructure, and disciplined service staff, we ensure perfection on every plate.
